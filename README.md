## About me

I am a Technical Artist focused on character rigging, technical workflows, and tool creation for Autodesk Maya. I build practical solutions that support animation and game-production pipelines.

## Education

I am a student at the [Faculty of Communication Sciences](https://comunicacion.udc.es/es), [University of A Coruña](https://www.udc.es/).

## Professional experience

### Character Rigger · The Violence Within
*Remote · Temporary contract · September 2025 – July 2026*

Designed, rigged, and optimized the skeleton and control systems for three complete characters in Autodesk Maya. Delivered game-ready rigs for an investor demo while working within a multidisciplinary team and a fixed production schedule.

## Selected projects

### [Inspector Sommers in: Ouroborian Madness](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4894260/Inspector_Sommers_in_Ouroborian_Madness/)
*Producer & Technical Artist · 2026*

Combined production and technical-art responsibilities: coordinated team task planning with Miro, programmed systems in Unreal Engine 5, and implemented menus and gameplay mechanics with Blueprints.

### Patacas
*Rigging & Character Animation · June 2025 – September 2025*

Collaborated with a student team on an animated short film. Researched and implemented the complete character rig, and animated its initial sequences.

### [Between Leaves](https://youtu.be/NWF7RV29SUM)
*Technical Art & Rigging · January 2025 – May 2025*

Worked in a nine-person team on an animated short film, contributing 3D modeling, shading, compositing, and character and prop rigging. Created Python automation tools to streamline the production workflow.

## Featured project

### [Maya Tools](https://github.com/davipazos/Maya-Tools/tree/Rigging)

A public selection of simple Autodesk Maya utilities I use for attribute-related tasks and day-to-day workflow support. Additional production work is kept private where appropriate.
